Chat Folders, Search & Export for Claude™ and for Gemini™ · Last updated: August 29, 2026

The product

Chat Folders, Search & Export for Claude™ and Chat Folders, Search & Export for Gemini™ (each “the extension”) are Chrome browser extensions that add folders, full-text search, statistics and export features for your own claude.ai or gemini.google.com conversation history. The core features are free. Claude Organizer Pro and Gemini Organizer Pro are optional one-time purchases ($29 each) that unlock additional features in the respective extension via a license key.
