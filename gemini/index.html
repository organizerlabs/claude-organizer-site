Chat Folders, Search & Export
for Gemini™

Gemini keeps every conversation, but finding one means scrolling an endless sidebar — and there is no way to export a chat. This Chrome extension fixes that: full-text search across every chat, folders & pins, stats, and Markdown / PDF / Obsidian export — with your images.

Add to Chrome — Free Privacy
🔍

Full-text search

Search the content of every chat, not just titles. Click a result to jump straight to that exact spot in the conversation.

📁

Folders & pins

Group chats into folders, pin the important ones on top. Gems are for personas — folders are for your history.

📝

Markdown export

One-click export of any conversation to clean Markdown. No paywall on basic export — ever.

📊

Your Gemini stats

Conversations, messages, words and your busiest month — computed locally, never uploaded.

Private by design. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your conversations are never uploaded anywhere — the extension talks only to gemini.google.com and, when you export a chat with images, to the Google hosts that serve those images. No analytics, no tracking, no account.
🛡 Built to last. The extension reads your chats through the same internal requests the Gemini web app uses instead of scraping the page, so typical interface redesigns are far less likely to break your folders, search or exports.
