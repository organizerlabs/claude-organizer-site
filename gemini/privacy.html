Privacy Policy

Chat Folders, Search & Export for Gemini™ · Last updated: August 28, 2026

Summary: everything runs locally in your browser. Your conversations never leave your machine.

What data the extension accesses

The extension runs only on gemini.google.com. Using the session you are already logged into, it reads the list and content of your own Gemini conversations in order to:

  • build a local search index (stored in your browser's IndexedDB),
  • display your chat list, folders and statistics,
  • generate Markdown/PDF/ZIP exports that are saved directly to your computer.

When you export with images enabled, the extension downloads the images that belong to your conversations from Google's image host (lh3.googleusercontent.com / lh3.google.com) into the export file. This is the only reason the extension asks for access to those hosts; nothing is uploaded there.

What data the extension stores

  • Locally in your browser only: the search index of your conversations, your folders, pins and preferences (IndexedDB and chrome.storage).
  • Nothing is stored on our servers. The extension has no servers.

What data the extension transmits

  • Your conversations: never. The only network requests related to your chats go to gemini.google.com itself — the same requests the website makes when you browse.
  • Pro license: payment is processed by our merchant of record (FastSpring) on their website. The license key you enter is verified locally inside the extension using cryptographic signatures — it is never sent anywhere. We never see your payment details.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies, no fingerprinting.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data (index, folders, preferences). You can also clear it anytime via Chrome's “Clear browsing data”.
